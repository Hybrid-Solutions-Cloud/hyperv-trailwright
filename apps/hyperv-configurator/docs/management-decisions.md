# Management decisions and explained options

Management requirements now combine the existing capability selections with thirteen additional decisions for prepared hosts, Azure VM operations/readiness, central fabric, production support, optimization, integrations, migration, scale, edge simplicity, software-defined operations and automation. The additional decisions distinguish unanswered, yes and no. Older capability selections are retained exactly.

Assign the architecture decision owner and record the chosen approach. If bare-metal OS deployment is required, choose VMM, a named OEM/external service or an operator and record the tool/version/qualification contract. VMM is not inferred solely from the bare-metal checkbox. Prepared hosts and bare metal may coexist when mapped to individual hosts.

`managementAdvisor.ts` derives candidates and explanations from selected requirements. VMM candidates arise from its selected provisioning role, private-cloud requirements, central fabric, optimization or the underlying Azure VM operation path. SCOM remains a separate monitoring choice. Azure VM operations need VMM plus Arc and conflict with offline operation or declined Azure readiness. Browser administration can use the Administration Mode candidate; Virtualization Mode stays excluded or under preview review according to the declared go-live requirements. Preview acceptance never establishes GA, HA, full feature support or an executable adapter.

Tenant interfaces, vendor integration versions and migration acceptance need their own records. A migration workstream is separate from permanent management-product selection. Scale and software-defined requirements produce explicit reviews rather than guessed product thresholds. Commercial notes direct the owner to existing coverage, separate SQL/access/HA rights and provider charges; no license assignment, included entitlement or price is created by advice.

Candidates do not add, remove or change components. Preserve the selected design in the inventory and link its dependencies, availability and licensing records. Validation findings focus the related decision field; unanswered decisions remain a visible review. JSON, management YAML, management-advice JSON/Markdown, the design PDF and Management decisions workbook/CSV retain the same inputs and rationale. Shared exports preserve categorical choices while scrubbing free-text evidence and owners.

Schema 11 adds an empty management decision object to schema 10 projects. It preserves all earlier booleans and context without guessing answers from prose. Strict parsing rejects unknown questions, choices and fields; a file claiming schema 10 cannot carry schema 11 decisions.
